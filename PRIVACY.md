# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

*Last updated: November 9, 2026*

## English

**RA2** does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, diagnostics, or device identifiers.

- **No Data Collection:** All game files imported by the user and all saved games remain strictly on your device inside the app's local storage (`Files` app).
- **No Accounts or Tracking:** The app requires no user account and includes no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.
- **Local Network:** When you choose to play a local network (LAN) match, the app communicates only with peers on your local network and never contacts any external server.

If you have any questions, please open an issue at <https://github.com/ViSH-App/RA2-Issues/issues>.

---

## 简体中文

**RA2** 不收集、不存储、不传输、也不共享任何个人数据、使用统计、诊断信息或设备标识符。

- **零数据收集：** 您导入的所有游戏数据文件及个人存档均完全保存在您设备本地沙盒（「文件」App）中，绝不会上传至任何服务器。
- **无账号与追踪：** 本应用无需注册登录，不包含任何第三方统计、广告 SDK 或追踪组件。
- **局域网联机：** 当您使用局域网联机功能时，应用仅在您所在的本地局域网内通信，不连接任何外部服务器。

如有任何疑问，请在 <https://github.com/ViSH-App/RA2-Issues/issues> 提交 Issue。
